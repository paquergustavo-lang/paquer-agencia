02Quanto preciso investir em anúncio?How much should I spend on ads?

03Em quanto tempo aparecem clientes?How soon will I get customers?

Eu não prometo número; prometo o trabalho e a transparência.Campaigns usually bring the first messages in week one, but results depend on your offer, area and budget. I don't promise numbers; I promise the work and transparency.
